# 2026.319.2359 — feat(validate)!: decode the IR opaque bodies before iterating them

## Summary

The **last** consumer leg of the candy IR opacity, on `opencharly/sdk` `v0.2026319.2349`. Closes **opencharly/plugin-box#81**.

`spec.CandyModel` now carries `service`, `volumes`, `aliases` and `data` as **opaque bytes** (the contract landed as `spec#331`/`#332`/`#333`/`#335`), so the validator — which read them as slices straight off the model — must decode first.

| where | change |
|---|---|
| `validate_graph.go` | `validatePackagedServices` and the orphan-packaged warning decode `m.Service` once per candy; `validateDataCandies` and the box-level data/volume checks decode `m.Data` / `m.Volumes` at the point of use |
| `validate_rules.go` | `validateVolume` and `validateAliases` decode once per candy |
| `validate_graph.go` (helpers) | `candyHasPackaged` / `candyHasOrphanPackaged` now take `[]spec.CandyService` instead of a whole `spec.CandyModel` — which keeps them PURE and makes the decode visible at the call site rather than hidden inside them |

**No new mechanism.** Every decode goes through `sdk/rawbody.Decode`, the crossing `sdk#538` consolidated, so this leg adds zero encoding logic and no third copy of anything.

**Decode once, at the point of use.** Each function decodes its candy's body into a local and iterates that; the two helpers that used to take a model now take the decoded list, so a caller cannot accidentally pass bytes where entries are expected.

## Change classification

Kind: a plugin-library change (the last consumer of a contract change). It breaks nothing for `charly` — this repo's API is its validator, and its inputs are unchanged.

**Breaking change + rollback:** the model's four fields are `RawBody` from spec `v0.2026319.2325`; a reader must decode. Rolling back reverts the pins and restores the slice reads; the validator's verdicts are identical either way.
